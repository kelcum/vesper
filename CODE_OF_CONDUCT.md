# Code of conduct

Vesper is a small project used by players of all ages. Everyone taking part,
in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is
expected to make that a good experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to software.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Keep criticism about code and ideas, never about people.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and explain why when they do.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual content or advances. Many Roblox players are children.
- Posting someone's private information, including account details.
- Requests or instructions for cheating, exploiting, account theft, or
  getting around Roblox's anti-cheat. Vesper doesn't do these things and the
  project won't help with them.
- Asking anyone to share passwords, cookies or tokens.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently ban
participants who break these rules. Report problems privately through
GitHub's reporting tools or the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
